# Native content: characters, room, animation clips

Everything in `native/assets/` is generated by scripts in `tools/model/` — nothing is hand-edited.
Contract: `docs/NATIVE_PLAN.md`. Coordinates in the files: glTF (metres, +Y up). Authoring happens in
Blender (Z-up, +Y toward the headboard); the exporter converts.

## Skin support maps (optional, `tools/model/native/skin_maps.py`)

All on the body's uv0 (same layout as the skin albedo), **linear** data (load without sRGB decode):

- `<who>_skin_mask.png` (1024², RGB):
  - **R = flush**: where blood shows first — cheeks, nose tip, ears, neck + décolleté, breasts (his chest),
    genitals. Use it as the weight of the existing `u_flush` tint: `base.rgb *= mix(1, flushTint, u_flush * mask.r)`
    (instead of tinting the whole body).
  - **G = translucency** = exp(−thickness / 10 mm), thickness ray-cast through the mesh: ears ≈ 0.6-0.9,
    nose/eyelids ≈ 0.3-0.5, fingers/toes ≈ 0.2-0.4, torso 0. Scale the back-lit `trans` term by it
    (`trans *= mask.g * 2.5`) so light bleeds through ears/fingers only, not through the torso.
  - **B = sweat / sheen**: forehead, temples, upper lip, neck, sternum, under the breasts, lower back. Multiply
    `u_sweat` by it (beads + roughness drop only where people actually sweat).
  - **A = arousal wetness** (Vivian): vestibule, inner labia, canal entrances, fading over the outer labia —
    roughness × (1 − 0.6 · wet · arousal) gives the moist sheen only where it belongs.
- `skin_detail_n.png` (512², tileable tangent-space normal, +Y up / OpenGL convention): pores + fine crosshatch.
  Sample at `uv0 * 48` (≈ 3.5 cm per tile on the body), blend with the 2K normal by adding xy
  (`n.xy += d.xy * 0.35 * (1 - mask.b * u_sweat)`), fade it out with distance (it only matters in close-ups).

## Vivian's hair (low chignon, `tools/model/female/hair_updo.py`)

Hair is pulled straight back from the hairline over an opaque scalp cap in three conforming card layers that
follow the head's meridians into a coiled bun low at the nape, plus two face-framing tendrils (temple → jaw,
outside the cheek) and two short nape wisps. Mesh `vivian_hair`, two primitives:

- `vivian_hair` (cards, ~41k quads): strand atlas `baseColorTexture` 2048², 8 columns (0-3 dense sheets, 4-5
  locks, 6-7 wisps). **V runs root (0) → tip (1) along every card, U across it**, so the strand tangent is
  dPos/dV (use it for anisotropic highlights). RGB = neutral strand luminance (dark roots → lighter tips),
  A = coverage with a clean cut (no haze below 0.08) → MASK 0.42, double-sided.
- `vivian_hair_cap` (scalp shell 1.6 mm off the skin): tileable 512² strand texture, UV repeats (wrap = REPEAT);
  its V also runs along the hair flow (toward the bun). Opaque in practice (A = 1).
- Colour: both carry `baseColorFactor` = authored espresso (linear 0.042, 0.015, 0.009); the game's hair
  presets multiply it (`kHair`), the texture stays neutral.
- Only the loose hair moves: everything except the tendrils/wisps is weighted 100 % to `頭`, so the swept
  sheets and the bun can never clip the body or cross the face. The armature node's `extras.afterglow`
  (`version 3`) holds `hairColliders` (spheres on head/face/neck/chest/breasts/shoulders, shrunk so no rest
  joint starts inside one) and `hairChains`: `[{root: "髪.0.a", kind: "tendril", stiffness, damping, gravity,
  maxAngleDeg}]` — tendrils 0.82 / 0.55 / 0.35 / 28°, wisps 0.70 / 0.55 / 0.35 / 40°. Honour `maxAngleDeg`
  (swing limit from the rest direction) so a tendril can never fall across her face, e.g. when she lies on her side.
- Bind height incl. hair stays within 1 % of 1.68 m (the bun sits low at the back), so the engine's 3 %
  auto-scale never triggers; native assets should not be rescaled at all.

`missionary.in, missionary.rub, missionary.embrace, legsup.in, doggy.in, doggy.rub, prone.in, cowgirl.in,
cowgirl.rub, revcowgirl.in, revcowgirl.rub, spoon.in, spoon.rub, oral.in, lick.in, solo.rub, solo.idle`, and the

lying face to face, lips together, her leg over his hip, hands on cheek/waist).

- `.in`: 25 frames, normalized time = penetration depth. Sample both characters at the same t. Her canal is
  aligned to his shaft in every frame (his `竿根` is aimed along her canal axis, his pelvis rides that axis);
  knees/hands are re-planted by IK each frame so nothing slides. `clips.json → insert_m` gives the shaft length
  inside her at t=0 and t=1: −6 → 104 mm for most positions; cowgirl 59 → 109 mm and reverse cowgirl 69 → 119 mm
  (she sits down onto him and never leaves him, so `vag_open` is 1 there); oral −4 → 71 mm into her mouth.
- Her body answers the depth inside the `.in` clips (k = smoothstep(0.45, 1, t)): missionary — her back arches,
  chin lifts, knees open, fists close in the sheet; doggy — head comes up, back dips, hands clutch; cowgirl —
  head falls back, she sits more upright; prone — fists tighten. Hands are relaxed by default (index straightest,
  pinky most curled) and grip sheets/his arms/his hair where it makes sense.
- Feet are posed by the sole, never by the ankle bone (which points 28° below the sole line): planted feet lie
  flat, kneeling feet rest on the instep with toes back, lying feet relax 20-30°. No more ballet points.
- `.rub`: 25-frame loop, only Vivian's right arm is keyed (`rubHand: "vivian.R"`): her fingertips circle on her clit.
- `missionary.embrace`: single pose layer (he goes down on his forearms, she holds his upper arms).
- `spoon.*`: both on their left sides, he lies behind her; she arches (pelvis tipped ~70°) so her canal opens
  toward him; bottom arms forward on the sheet, feet on their outer edges, his top hand on her hip.
- `lick.in`: he lies on his front between her knees, chest raised on his elbows, hands holding her knees apart;
  her right hand rests in his hair, the left grips the sheet; t is the tongue stroke (16 mm). He is flaccid here
  (`aroused` 0, shaft tucked under him).
- `solo.*`: Marcus stands beside the bed (`marcusHidden: true` — hide him or keep him as a watcher).
- Hair chains and eyes are never keyed: simulate the hair (colliders in the armature node's
  `extras.afterglow.hairColliders`) and drive the eyes from gaze.
- **Hands really touch.** Every intended hand contact is solved against the posed skin of its target: palms land
  on the surface and each finger curls until its pad meets the curve (breasts, thighs, hips, the shaft, his
  chest, his hair); fingertip rubbing keeps the pads on the skin with the palm lifted off the mons, the ring and
  little finger tucked. The elbow is chosen so the wrist stays inside its range (the forearm does the turning)
  and never drops into the mattress. Then the real skinned hand is checked and lifted out if it sank in.
- **Joint ranges** (rigkit `ROM`, swing/twist from the anatomical rest): neck 40°/45°, head 32°/38°, wrist
  65°/85° (twist = forearm pronation), ankle incl. midfoot 82°/35°. Every keyed frame is clamped to them.
- **Contacts for touch simulation** (`clips.json → contacts`): one track per hand / mouth that touches skin —
  `{char, effector, bone, offset, radius, target {char, bone, region}, gap_mm [min, max], t [first, last]}`. The
  contact point each frame = the effector bone's world matrix × `offset` (bone-local, like `fpv.offset_local`);
  `region` names the soft tissue to deform (`breast.L`, `vulva`, `hips`, `thigh.R`, `chest`, `genitals`, `head`…).
- **Soft bones** (armature `extras.afterglow.softBones`): his scrotum (`玉袋`, sag + swing) and her breasts
  (`乳親.L/R`, light jiggle) as damped springs around their animated pose — `{bone, stiffness, damping, gravity,
  maxAngleDeg}`, same solver as the hair tips.

## Outfits (`tools/model/native/outfits.py`)

Each garment is the matching patch of the character's own skin, lifted 2–7 mm off it, carrying the body's skin
weights and every body morph (same target names), so it follows every clip, pose and body shape exactly
(a skin-tight cut — no cloth simulation needed, nothing can clip through it). One GLB per style with the
armature + two meshes `<who>_outfit_<style>_outer` / `_under`, each with node extras
`afterglow.outfit = {style, layer, hidesWhen}` (`outer` hides when `partial`/`nude`, `under` when `nude`). Bind
them to the character's skeleton by joint name and apply the character's morph weights to them.
Vivian: `evening_dress` (wine satin bodycon dress, low back · black lace bra + briefs), `knit_sweater_jeans`
(sweater + jeans as one fitted layer · nude-tone cotton set), `sundress_bikini` (cream cotton sundress · blue
bikini). Marcus: `flannel_jeans`, `suit_open_collar`, `linen_shirt_shorts` (outer = shirt + trousers/shorts,
under = boxer briefs). His genitals are not part of the garments: hide the `marcus_genital` primitive while his
`under` layer is shown.

## Room (`tools/model/room/build_room.py` → `post.mjs`)

Real-scale night bedroom: mattress 1.80 × 2.00 m, top (sheet) at y = 0.604, bed centre (0, 0.604, −1.22), head
toward −Z — exactly the bed the clips are authored on (`tools/model/anim/scene.py`). Everything that could get in
the way of a body is kept out of the play area: the pillows stand propped against the headboard (`playArea.zMin`
in the extras: no clip goes past z = −1.98), the duvet lies kicked off on the floor left of the bed, nightstands
are beside the bed. Lighting is a Cycles bake (diffuse direct + indirect, 1024 spp, angle-based islands packed
across all lit objects) stored sRGB in `lightmap` and wired as each lit material's `emissiveTexture` on
**texCoord 1** with emissive factor 1 → engine: room colour = base × emissive(uv1) × `lightmap.scale` (1.67).
Lights (`KHR_lights_punctual`, engine units): `lamp_key` spot 120 (→ /120), `lamp_0` point 0.55, `candle_0..3`
points 0.12 (flicker by name), `moon` directional 0.45. Scene `extras.afterglow` (= room.json): `bedTop`,
`bedCenter`, `bed {center, size, headZ, footZ}`, `lamp`, `lights`, `lightmap`, `cameraBounds`, `window`,
`playArea`. Every clip is verified against the real room furniture (headboard, pillows, duvet, nightstands,
lamps): zero contacts.

## How it is built (any Linux box with ~8 GB RAM + swap works; the team box has 20 cores)

```
bash tools/model/native/setup_toolchain.sh  # Blender 4.2.3 + MPFB 2.0.18 + MakeHuman CC0 assets into /root/agb
bash tools/model/native/run_chars.sh        # Vivian (build5 → hair_updo → rig_export @1.68 m) then Marcus (build → genitals → paint → hair → rig_export @1.83 m)
blender -b /root/agb/out/vivian_game.blend --python tools/model/female/render_hair2.py -- <dir> front,three_q,back,top   # hair check renders
blender -b /root/agb/male/out/marcus_game.blend --python tools/model/native/prepare.py -- marcus
blender -b /root/agb/out/vivian_game.blend      --python tools/model/native/prepare.py -- vivian
xvfb-run blender -b --python tools/model/anim/author.py [-- clip,clip --norender]  # bake + verify, previews in /root/agb/native/previews
                                                    # (no GL on the box: AG_PREVIEW_ENGINE=CYCLES renders the previews with Cycles)
                                                    # (a partial run keeps the other clips' actions in anim.blend)
blender -b /root/agb/native/anim.blend --python tools/model/native/export_chars.py
python3 tools/model/native/textures.py && node tools/model/native/post.mjs <raw.glb> <out.glb> <who> /root/agb/native/tex/<who>
blender -b /root/agb/out/vivian_s4.blend --python tools/model/native/skin_maps.py -- vivian      # skin masks + detail normal
blender -b /root/agb/male/out/marcus_b.blend --python tools/model/native/skin_maps.py -- marcus
bash tools/model/native/stage_assets.sh     # copy into native/assets + regenerate clips.json
BAKE_RES=2048 BAKE_SAMPLES=1024 blender -b --python tools/model/room/build_room.py   # room + Cycles lightmap (~2 min on 20 cores)
AG_ROOM_STYLE=cabin|villa|hotel AG_ROOM_OUT=<dir> blender -b --python tools/model/room/build_room.py   # story bedrooms
blender -b /root/agb/native/<who>_native.blend --python tools/model/native/outfits.py -- <who> <out_dir>
node tools/model/room/post.mjs /root/agb/native/room/room_raw.glb /root/agb/native/room/room.json /root/agb/native/room
```

Animation authoring (`tools/model/anim/`): `rigkit.py` is a deterministic pose solver (armature-space FK, anatomical
orient/aim, analytic two-bone IK that keeps each limb's rest hinge, palm-aware hands, finger curl, direct keyframing);
`body.py` wraps it per character; `positions.py` / `positions2.py` define every clip; `scene.py` loads both
characters + the room/bed, settles bodies on the mattress from the evaluated mesh and runs the BVH checks;
`author.py` bakes, verifies (`/root/agb/native/verify.json`) and renders previews. Where a pose has several
reasonable layouts (her knee width in cowgirl, his straddle vs. kneeling between her legs in prone, where her
hands go in the embrace, his hands in lick and reverse cowgirl) `positions.best_of` poses each candidate, scores
it with the same contact/floor check and keeps the cleanest one; the choice is made once per clip at mid-stroke
so it never changes between frames. Limbs are always placed by their real world side (`sides()`), never by the
L/R label, and the measured canal axis is kept in the sagittal plane (the raw PCA axis leaned 3.8°).

## Verification

Each clip is checked at t = 0, .25, .5, .75, 1 (numbers in `clips.json` → `verify` + `contacts`, raw data in
`/root/agb/native/verify.json`): intersecting triangle pairs between the two bodies outside the intended contact,
the deepest such penetration, each body's lowest point vs. the mattress top (mm; − = pressed into the soft
mattress), contacts with the real room's furniture (`room_hit` names the piece), how far any limited joint goes
past its range (0: every keyed frame is clamped), the largest per-bone step between frames, and every hand
contact's skin gap (min..max over the clip; 0 = touching, − = pressing in). V/M = Vivian/Marcus, L/R = hand.
Close-ups of every hand contact are rendered by `tools/model/anim/render_hands.py` and checked by eye.

Intended contact (not counted): genitals (her `v_*` masks × his shaft/scrotum + pubic mound), hands on anything,
her mouth (oral) / his mouth (lick), his shaft vs. her inner thighs and buttocks within 6 cm of the entrance,
pelvis-to-pelvis flesh (her thighs/buttocks within 26 cm of the entrance × his groin, hips, upper thighs and lower
belly within 28 cm of the shaft root; its depth is tracked as `pelvic_depth_mm` and the layout search penalises
anything past 8 mm), her breasts × his shaft in titjob, torso/hip contact in the story clips.

scores it with the same checks at **both** the top (t = 0) and the bottom (t = 0.85) of the stroke; the choice is
made once per clip, so it never changes between frames.
