# 2026-09-12 — Goat-chan solo: hands + legs fixed

## What was actually wrong (two independent bugs)

### 1. The shipped `goatchan.glb` was corrupt
`scratch/blender/fix_legs_and_hands.py` (an earlier session) had re-exported the
model with `export_morph=False`. That one flag:
- **corrupted the vertex normals of 1296 hand vertices** (vector error up to
  1.81 of a max 2.0 → ~130°); all damage was inside `y 0.67–0.76, |x| 0.40–0.47`
  = the hands, 29–33% of hand verts;
- **dropped all 17 morph targets** (shape keys `nipples/pattern/extraboob`).
- Geometry, weights and topology were untouched (`POSITION` maxDelta 0.004,
  `WEIGHTS_0` maxDelta 0). So the script achieved nothing except breaking
  the normals.

The same script's step 7 ("thicken hands") also has a Python precedence bug —
`(side=="L" and x) or (side=="R" and x) and z and y` — so the L branch ignores
the y/z filters and selects the whole forearm.

**Fixed** by restoring the model and adding a reproducible pipeline:
- `scratch/blender/export_glb.py` — exports from the pristine `.blend`
  (which is byte-identical to `E:\Downloads\ReleaseVer\goatchan.blend`),
  selecting only meshes that carry an Armature modifier. Reproduces the
  shipped mesh exactly: identical accessor signature, 0/26435 bad normals.
- `scratch/blender/audit_glb.py`, `scratch/glb/cmp_all_normals.py`,
  `cmp_attrs.py`, `check_normals_health.py`, `diff_glb.py` — regression gates.
- **Never export this model with `export_morph=False`.**

### 2. The rig never drove two auxiliary leg bones
`glbBuildRig` walks `GLB_RIG_MAP`, which had only 足/ひざ/足首/つま先. But the
skeleton also contains:
- `ひざALT.L/R` — parent is **足 (the THIGH)**, not ひざ;
- `足捩.L/R` — parent is **下半身**, not 足.

Neither was driven, so they held their bind rotation while the real leg bent.
`diag-weights` showed the sock shin verts carry `ひざALT 5% + 足捩 7%`, so those
verts were pulled between two divergent transforms → the flat "blade" shin.

**Fixed** by mapping each to its real counterpart (`thigh: true` flag reuses the
thigh's absolute+mirrored-spread formula). Measured with `diag-collapse`:
thigh cross-section ratio `[_,1.23,1.48,2.38]` → `[_,1.00,1.00,1.006]`.

## Hands were not a mesh problem
Rendering the rig with arms forced to bind (`scratch/force-bind-arms.js`) shows
a properly rounded hand with a visible thumb. The "thin hand" was just
**finger curl too low**: at 0.15–0.42 the MMD fingers stay nearly straight, so
from any near-side camera the hand reads as a flat splayed paddle. Raised the
solo / planted / caress / cup curl values to 0.26–0.72 (≈0.6 = relaxed hand).

## Hands sitting off the body (fixed in a second pass)

The work state blamed "IK loop dynamics". It was two separate geometric bugs in
`aimArmAt3`:

1. **The upper arm was aimed at the target.** That puts the elbow ON the
   shoulder→target line, so the forearm can only span `|dist − upper|`. The
   wrist lands on the right sphere at the wrong angle, missing by
   `≈ 2·dist·sin(α/2)` with `cos α = (u²+d²−f²)/(2ud)`. The MMD girl's torso is
   short, so the breast/mons sit close to the shoulder, α is large, and the
   predicted misses (0.148 m, 0.196 m) matched the measured ones (0.158 m,
   0.210 m) almost exactly. The upper arm must point at the *true elbow*.

2. **The twist correction was degenerate.** With the upper arm aimed at the
   target, the "predicted" and "desired" forearm directions were both parallel
   to the aim, so their perpendicular components were ~0 and the computed twist
   was always 0 — no correction ever happened, and the fold plane was whatever
   minimal-arc aiming produced.

Fix, in one block:
- pole `p` = which side the elbow bends, read from the **live elbow offset**
  (stable frame to frame, and it matches the flexion sign automatically);
- `α` from the cosine rule, applied as a rotation about `dir × p`;
- twist about the elbow direction until the elbow's local X equals
  `elbowDir × foreDir`. **Use the exact hinge axis, not a plane normal** — a
  normal is sign-ambiguous and picking wrong folds one forearm 90° out of plane
  (0.36 m miss on one arm only).

Result: `|wrist − target|` 0.158/0.210 m → **0.000/0.000 m**.

Also removed `bendBias = ∓0.22 rad`, which shifted the elbow off the
cosine-rule angle (reach 0.171 m for a 0.133 m target).

Then `GLB_LAND_OFF`: these are **world** offsets and she lies on her back, so
`+Y` is outward from her chest. `乳親` is the breast's ROOT at the chest wall,
not its surface, so 0.11 m left the target only ~0.12 m from the shoulder and
the arm had to fold almost completely. Swept: 0.11 m tight / **0.19 m relaxed
and still touching** / 0.27 m visibly floating. Now breast 0.19, mons 0.15/0.10.

`aimArmAt3` is shared with the procedural rigs (`restDir = -Y`) — re-shot yuki,
mara and sable to confirm no regression.

## Traps that cost time today

- **`elbow.rotation.x` == the achieved interior angle**, so the "achieved"
  angle from `acos(u·f)` is its *supplement* (the vector `(E−S)` is the negative
  of the triangle side). I chased a phantom elbow bug for a while because of
  this.
- Live debug overrides persist across reloads of the *same* page but not across
  a real reload — and `glbSetGain(part, 0)` FREEZES rather than resets.
- The static server and verify daemon die between turns; start them inside the
  same turn as the work (or use `verify-fast.mjs`, which spawns its own Chrome).
- **`git pack-refs --all` (and a bad `printf`) can leave the branch ref broken**
  or containing the literal `HEAD`. Always recover from `.git/logs/HEAD`: the
  second field of the last line is the new commit SHA.
